Routine Library subscriptions

Pro includes the routines marked Pro. Ultra includes all Pro routines and the routines marked Ultra. The selected plan and recurring price are shown before payment. Access begins after the first payment is confirmed.

Monobank charges the amount in UAH shown before checkout, then repeats that charge monthly until you cancel. Your card issuer may convert the amount to another currency.

An active Pro member can make a one-time $10 USD payment for Ultra access until the end of the current paid period. Your account shows the upgrade price and end date before opening the Monobank payment page. You can save your choice of Pro or Ultra for the next month. Saving a choice turns off the current automatic renewal. After the paid period ends, confirm a new monthly subscription in Monobank to continue on the selected plan.

To stop future charges, sign in to your Routine Pack account, open Your account, and choose Cancel auto-renewal in your subscription section. Cancelling turns off future charges. You can still watch the videos and download files included in your plan until the end of the paid monthly period shown in your account, even if you cancel immediately. After that, paid videos and downloads require an active subscription again.
